Terms, refunds and privacy

Last updated: October 8, 2026. Questions? Write to support@hammertimepiano.store.

Terms of sale

What you buy, and from whom

Who sells what

HammerTime Piano is a free app. HammerTime Piano Plus is an optional purchase: a license key that unlocks the Plus features listed on the home page. HammerTime Piano is published from Quebec, Canada.

Who takes the payment

The sale goes through Polar (polar.sh), which acts as the official reseller (the "merchant of record"). Polar takes your payment, works out the taxes that apply in your country and sends you your receipt. Polar's terms also apply to your payment.

Price and payment

The price is in US dollars (USD). The final total, taxes included, is shown at checkout before you pay. A promo code is entered at checkout.

Your license

You receive a personal license key by email, starting with HAMMERTIME-. It never expires. It activates on up to 3 devices; to free a spot, remove the key from the device you're leaving, in the app.

The key is for your personal use: don't publish it and don't resell it. A key that circulates publicly can be revoked.

A beta app

HammerTime Piano is in beta: the app keeps evolving, and the list of Plus features may grow. If Plus doesn't work for you, write to us.

Your rights

These terms add to the rights the law gives you as a consumer and don't take any of them away. They are governed by the laws of Quebec and the federal laws of Canada that apply there.

Refunds

You have 30 days to change your mind

If HammerTime Piano Plus isn't for you, you can ask for a full refund within 30 days of your purchase. You don't have to give a reason.

How to ask

Write to support@hammertimepiano.store with the email you bought with. The refund is made through Polar, to the payment method you used. How long the money takes to show up depends on your bank or card.

After the refund

Your key is revoked. Plus locks on your devices the next time the app checks your key online. The free features keep working.

A problem before you refund?

If the key won't activate or Plus doesn't work on your device, write to us first: we'd rather make it work. After the 30 days, write to us anyway, for example about a key that won't activate or a double purchase: we look at each case.

Privacy

What stays with you, and what goes out

On your device

Your MIDI files, settings, corrections and history stay on your device. The app has no account, no analytics and doesn't upload any of your files.

When the app talks to the Internet

The app works offline. It only contacts a server for your Plus key, and only Polar's (api.polar.sh): to activate it, to check it again (at most once every 7 days, when you're online) and to remove it.

These exchanges contain your key, the identifiers of the activation at Polar and a device name: "HammerTime Piano" followed by a short code the app generates itself. They contain none of your MIDI files, none of your settings and not your name. As with any Internet exchange, Polar sees your device's IP address. Without a network, a key that is already activated keeps working.

The app keeps the email linked to your purchase on your device, to show it on the license screen.

When you buy

Polar collects what the payment needs: email, country and payment method. We receive your email and your order details from Polar, to send you your key, help you and refund you if needed. We don't see your card number. Polar keeps its data under its own privacy policy and its accounting and tax obligations.

When you give feedback

"Give feedback", in the app, opens a GitHub page in your browser with the text already filled in. Nothing is sent until you post the message, and you need a GitHub account to do it. Writing to support@hammertimepiano.store works too.

This site

This site uses no analytics, no tracking cookies and no external fonts. It only remembers the language you picked, in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and downloads come from GitHub: GitHub may keep connection logs, under its own policy.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted by writing to support@hammertimepiano.store. That is also the contact for the person in charge of protecting personal information.

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes too.
